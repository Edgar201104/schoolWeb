Admissions

Applying is simple. Follow these steps, then contact the office with any questions.

  1. Send an inquiryContact the school office.
  2. Fill in the applicationCollect the form at the office.
  3. Submit documentsSee the list below.
  4. Meet the staffShort interview with the student and family.
  5. Receive a decisionThe school will contact you.
  6. EnrollComplete enrollment and fees.
